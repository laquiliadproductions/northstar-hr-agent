# Northstar Analytics International Remote Work Policy

*Temporary work outside the United States approval and compliance requirements*

| **Policy owner** | People Operations | **Version** | 1.0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Effective date** | September 26 2026 | **Applies to** | United States employees |

## 1 Purpose and Scope

Northstar Analytics permits temporary work from another country only when it is approved in advance and can be performed lawfully, securely, and without unacceptable business or client risk. Employees must not perform any work outside the United States until they receive written approval for the specific country, location, and dates. Reading email, joining a meeting, accessing a system, or completing a brief task counts as work.

This policy applies to United States employees who wish to remain in their U.S. role and payroll while temporarily working abroad. It does not cover company-directed business travel without a personal remote-work extension, permanent relocation or assignment, or personal travel during which no work is performed. A permanent move requires a separate employment and relocation review.

## 2 Definitions

- **Home country.** The country in which the employee is employed and paid by Northstar; for this policy, the United States.
- **Host country.** The country from which the employee proposes to work temporarily.
- **International remote work.** Performing company duties from a host country while retaining a U.S.-based role and payroll.

## 3 Eligibility and Limits

Eligibility depends on the employee's standing, duties, client obligations, proposed location, security requirements, and the ability to maintain reliable performance and coverage. Approval is discretionary unless required by law. It does not create a right to future international work or change the employee's assigned work location.

- **Advance request.** Submit a complete request at least 45 calendar days before departure. Late requests may be denied without review.
- **Duration.** International remote work is ordinarily limited to 30 calendar days total in a rolling 12-month period. Longer periods require executive approval and all required legal, tax, security, and client reviews.
- **Specific approval.** Approval covers only the stated dates and location. Employees may not extend the period, change countries, or substitute a location without new written approval.
- **Restricted locations.** Northstar will deny work from locations affected by sanctions, export controls, unacceptable safety or security risk, client restrictions, or legal and operational barriers.

## 4 Required Request Information

The request must include the proposed dates; country, city, and work address; itinerary and purpose; time zone and work schedule; duties, systems, and data needed; citizenship, passport, visa, and work-authorization information; prior presence or tax-residency history in the host country; emergency contacts; and equipment, connectivity, and workspace plans. The employee must promptly provide accurate follow-up information requested for the review.

## 5 Review and Approval Factors

People Operations coordinates the review. The employee may work abroad only after receiving a written decision that identifies any conditions or limitations.

- **Manager.** Confirms role suitability, performance, staffing, client commitments, meeting coverage, deliverables, and the proposed schedule.
- **People Operations.** Reviews employment-law, payroll, benefits, workers' compensation, leave, and location-record implications.
- **Legal and Finance.** Assess tax, corporate-presence, regulatory, sanctions, export-control, insurance, and contractual risk when needed.
- **Information Security and Privacy.** Review device, network, data-localization, cross-border transfer, system-access, and threat conditions.
- **Client or data owner.** Provides approval when contracts, data rights, regulated information, or access rules require it.

A request may be denied when compliance cannot be confirmed, the cost or risk is unreasonable, client permission is unavailable, or the arrangement would disrupt operations. A verbal discussion, prior approval, calendar entry, or manager's informal support is not authorization.

## 6 Immigration Tax and Legal Compliance

The employee must have lawful immigration status and any visa, work authorization, registration, or permit required in the host country. Entry as a tourist or visa-free visitor does not necessarily authorize work. The employee must follow all entry, departure, and reporting rules and cooperate with Northstar's immigration, employment, payroll, and tax review.

Northstar may require advice from its professional advisers and may deny or condition a request to avoid individual or corporate tax exposure, payroll or social-insurance duties, local-employment obligations, or establishment of a taxable business presence. Northstar does not provide personal legal or tax advice. Unless law or a written agreement requires otherwise, the employee is responsible for personal filings, taxes, penalties, permits, and professional advice arising from personal travel or international remote work.

## 7 Information Security Privacy and Data Access

Employees must use only Northstar-approved devices, accounts, multifactor authentication, secure access methods, and storage locations. Public or shared computers, shared accounts, unapproved removable media, and local printing or storage of company or client information are prohibited. Work must occur in a private setting over a trusted network; access to sensitive systems or data may be limited or disabled while abroad.

Employees must follow the Data Classification and Handling Standard, Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy, and applicable client requirements. Immediately report a lost, stolen, seized, searched, or inspected device; a request by border or government authorities for access; or any suspected exposure or unauthorized access. Do not obstruct authorities. Protect personal safety, comply with lawful instructions, and contact Information Security and Legal as soon as practical.

## 8 Schedule Performance and Timekeeping

Employees must remain available during the approved hours, meet client and team commitments, and maintain accurate calendars. Unless the written approval states otherwise, home-team hours and deadlines continue to apply. Nonexempt employees must record all time worked, take required meal and rest periods, and obtain advance approval for overtime. Working abroad does not change pay, benefits, leave, performance standards, or other employment terms.

## 9 Costs Insurance Health and Safety

Unless Northstar requires the travel or approves an exception in writing, the employee is responsible for airfare, lodging, visas, permits, personal tax advice, internet, coworking space, insurance, medical care, evacuation, and other travel or remote-work costs. Approval does not create an allowance, pay differential, or reimbursement entitlement.

Employees must confirm that appropriate health, travel, property, and liability coverage is available and understand that company benefits may have geographic limits. They must monitor official safety and public-health guidance, maintain emergency contact information, and take reasonable precautions. Northstar may require the employee to stop working, relocate, return home, or use available leave when safety, legal, client, or operational conditions change.

## 10 Approval Procedure

| Step | Required action |
|---|---|
| **1 Submit request** | At least 45 days before departure, provide all information listed in Section 4 to the manager and People Operations. |
| **2 Manager screening** | The manager evaluates performance, role suitability, coverage, client needs, and the proposed schedule. |
| **3 Cross-functional review** | People Operations obtains Legal, Finance, Information Security, Privacy, client, or data-owner review as required. |
| **4 Written decision** | The employee receives approval, conditional approval, or denial. Approval specifies location, dates, hours, access, and other conditions. |
| **5 Predeparture confirmation** | Before travel, the employee confirms authorization, equipment, secure access, emergency contacts, and that no material facts have changed. |

## 11 Changes Revocation and Emergencies

The employee must disclose changes to dates, location, itinerary, immigration status, duties, data access, health or safety conditions, or client requirements before the change occurs. Northstar may modify or revoke approval at any time when legal, tax, security, client, safety, performance, or operational concerns arise. The employee must then stop working from the host country and follow instructions to return, relocate, or use available leave.

In an emergency, the employee should first protect personal safety and contact local emergency services when appropriate, then notify the manager and People Operations as soon as practical. Information-security incidents must also be reported immediately under the Security Incident Reporting Procedure.

## 12 Compliance and Reporting

Working internationally without approval, providing incomplete or inaccurate information, or violating an approval condition may result in loss of remote-work privileges and corrective action, up to and including separation. Northstar prohibits retaliation for raising a good-faith legal, tax, safety, security, wage-hour, or workplace concern. Concerns may be reported to a manager, another manager, People Operations, Information Security, or Legal as appropriate.
