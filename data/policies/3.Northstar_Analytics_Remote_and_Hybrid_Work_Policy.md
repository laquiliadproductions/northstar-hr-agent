# Northstar Analytics Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

*Eligibility scheduling workspace security and approval requirements*

| Policy owner | People Operations | Version | 1.0 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Effective date | September 26 2026 | Applies to | United States employees |

## 1 Purpose and Scope

Northstar Analytics supports remote and hybrid work when it serves client commitments, secure operations, collaboration, and effective performance. This policy applies to United States employees whose approved arrangement includes recurring work away from a Northstar or client site. Remote work is not an entitlement or guarantee unless required as a reasonable accommodation or by law.

Remote means the employee regularly works from an approved offsite location. Hybrid means the employee divides work between an approved offsite location and a Northstar or client site. Occasional remote work is a short-term manager-approved exception and does not change the employee's assigned arrangement.

## 2 Eligibility and Approval

People Operations and the manager determine eligibility based on role duties, client and contract requirements, data access, equipment, collaboration needs, performance, time-zone coverage, tax and employment obligations, and the proposed location. New or modified arrangements require written approval before they begin.

- **Employee standing.** The employee must meet current performance, conduct, attendance, security, and timekeeping expectations.
- **Approved location.** The employee must work in the approved U.S. state and report any recurring or temporary location change before working there.
- **International work.** Working from another country is prohibited without advance approval under the International Remote Work Policy.
- **Accommodation requests.** People Operations evaluates disability, pregnancy, religion, and other legally protected accommodation requests through the interactive process.
## 3 Schedule Attendance and Timekeeping

Employees must follow the approved schedule, attend required onsite days, maintain accurate calendars, and remain reachable during team or client collaboration hours. The manager may set core hours and meeting expectations that are reasonable for the role and approved time zone. Work location does not change pay, attendance, leave, or conduct requirements unless confirmed in writing.

Nonexempt employees must record all time worked and required meal periods, obtain advance approval for overtime, and never work off the clock. All employees must report outages, delays, absences, or a need to change location as soon as practical. Remote work is not a substitute for regular dependent care, although brief or emergency interruptions should be discussed with the manager.

## 4 Workspace and Safety

Employees must maintain a reasonably safe, professional, and functional workspace with reliable connectivity, appropriate lighting and ergonomics, and protection from unauthorized viewing or listening. The workspace must support confidential calls and client meetings. Employees must immediately report work-related injuries, hazards, theft, or property damage to the manager and People Operations; emergency services should be contacted first when immediate safety is at risk.

## 5 Information Security and Privacy

Remote and hybrid employees must follow the Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy and Data Classification and Handling Standard. Only approved accounts, devices, storage locations, networks, and collaboration tools may be used for company business.

- **Secure access.** Use multifactor authentication, approved secure access methods, strong credentials, and screen locking; never share accounts or authentication prompts.
- **Private work.** Prevent household members and others from viewing, hearing, handling, or using company or client information and equipment.
- **Data handling.** Do not store company data on personal devices, send it to personal accounts, or print restricted information without authorization and secure disposal.
- **Incident reporting.** Immediately report phishing, loss, theft, misdirected information, unauthorized access, malware, or suspected exposure under the Security Incident Reporting Procedure.
Employees should not expect personal privacy when using company systems, accounts, networks, or devices. Northstar may access, preserve, review, or monitor company resources for security, support, legal, compliance, and business-continuity purposes, subject to applicable law.

## 6 Equipment and Expenses

Northstar determines the equipment appropriate for each role. Company equipment remains company property and must be protected, maintained, used only by authorized persons, and returned under the Company Equipment Lifecycle Policy and Procedures. Employees may not disable security controls, install unapproved software, or repair or dispose of equipment outside approved processes.

Northstar reimburses required business expenses in accordance with the Business Expense Reimbursement Policy and applicable law. Employees must obtain approval before purchasing equipment, furniture, internet upgrades, coworking access, or other remote-work services. Ordinary household costs are not reimbursable unless approved or legally required.

## 7 Communication Performance and Conduct

Remote and hybrid employees are held to the same performance, quality, conduct, confidentiality, and client-service standards as other employees. They must communicate availability, meet deadlines, document decisions, participate in required meetings, and raise workload or delivery risks early. Managers must evaluate outcomes consistently and may not equate physical visibility with performance.

- **Professional presence.** Use appropriate audio, video, background, dress, and meeting practices for the audience and work.
- **Approved channels.** Conduct business through approved systems and preserve records in designated repositories.
- **Onsite participation.** Attend scheduled onsite work, training, client sessions, or events when directed with reasonable notice.
- **Respectful workplace.** All conduct and anti-harassment standards apply in messages, video meetings, calls, coworking spaces, and work-related events.
## 8 Changes Interruptions and Business Continuity

Employees must have a reasonable continuity plan for short power, internet, or workspace disruptions. They should notify the manager promptly, relocate to an approved secure location when practical, or use available leave when work cannot continue. Northstar may temporarily require onsite work or another approved location because of security events, outages, client needs, training, investigations, or operational emergencies.

A recurring arrangement may be modified or ended for business, performance, attendance, security, legal, tax, client, or workspace reasons. Northstar will ordinarily provide at least 10 business days' notice when practical, but may act immediately when risk or operational need requires. Ending a discretionary arrangement does not by itself constitute discipline or a change in employment status.

## 9 Request and Review Procedure

| Step | Required action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Employee request | Submit the proposed schedule, primary work address, state, time zone, workspace plan, connectivity, and requested start date. |
| 2 Manager assessment | Evaluate role suitability, team and client coverage, performance, security, equipment, onsite needs, and measurable expectations. |
| 3 Functional review | People Operations confirms employment and tax considerations; Information Security, Legal, Finance, or the client owner reviews when needed. |
| 4 Written decision | Approval states the arrangement, location, schedule, onsite expectations, equipment, expenses, review date, and any conditions. |
| 5 Ongoing review | The manager reviews effectiveness after 60 days and periodically thereafter; changes must be documented before implementation. |

A manager may not approve work from a different state or country without People Operations review. No arrangement is effective based only on a verbal discussion, calendar entry, or prior practice.

## 10 Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibilities |
| --- | --- |
| Employee | Maintain the approved location and schedule; protect data and equipment; communicate reliably; record time accurately; report changes, incidents, and injuries. |
| Manager | Set measurable expectations; review requests consistently; maintain coverage; monitor results; protect confidential information; escalate legal, security, or employee-relations concerns. |
| People Operations | Administer approvals, location changes, accommodations, tax and employment reviews, documentation, exceptions, and consistency checks. |
| Information Security | Define technical safeguards, approve secure access, advise on data handling, and respond to security incidents. |

## 11 Compliance and Reporting

Failure to follow an approved arrangement or this policy may result in modification or withdrawal of remote-work privileges and corrective action, up to and including separation. Northstar prohibits retaliation for requesting an accommodation, reporting a safety or security concern, raising a wage-hour issue, or exercising another protected right. Concerns may be reported to a manager, another manager, People Operations, or Information Security as appropriate.
