## Employee Acknowledgment

## How to use this handbook

This handbook is the starting point for understanding employment practices, workplace expectations, and available resources at Northstar Analytics. Employees are expected to read it, follow the standards that apply to their work, ask questions when guidance is unclear, and consult the referenced policies for detailed procedures.

Northstar may revise, suspend, or replace policies as business needs or legal requirements change. People Operations will communicate material updates and may require employees to acknowledge them. Nothing in this handbook restricts employees from discussing wages, hours, or working conditions; reporting possible legal violations; participating in government investigations; or exercising any other legally protected right.

## 1 Handbook Purpose and Use

The Employee Handbook establishes the companywide baseline for how Northstar Analytics manages employment and how employees are expected to work with colleagues, clients, company systems, and data. It applies across office, remote, hybrid, client, and work-related event settings, subject to applicable law and any approved local supplement.

### Scope

Unless a section states otherwise, this handbook applies to all Northstar Analytics employees. Independent contractors, agency workers, interns, and other nonemployees must follow the security, conduct, confidentiality, and site rules that apply to their assignments, but this handbook does not change their legal status.

### Relationship to other policies

This handbook summarizes subjects that are governed in more detail by separate policies, standards, guides, and procedures. The more specific document controls for operational details such as eligibility, approval steps, deadlines, security controls, and required forms. People Operations can identify the current version when documents appear inconsistent.

### Employee responsibilities

- **Read and comply.** Review this handbook and all policies relevant to the employee's role, location, systems, data, and client work.

- **Ask before acting.** Seek guidance from a manager, People Operations, Information Security, Legal, or another designated owner when requirements are unclear.

- **Complete required actions.** Meet training, acknowledgment, reporting, documentation, and approval deadlines.

- **Report concerns promptly.** Use the channels in this handbook for suspected misconduct, harassment, safety hazards, data exposure, or other policy concerns.

- **Protect against retaliation.** Do not retaliate against anyone who raises a concern, requests an accommodation, participates in a review, or exercises a protected right.

## 2 About Northstar Analytics

Northstar Analytics is a business intelligence and data consulting company that helps organizations transform complex information into clear, actionable insights. Our distributed team of data analysts, engineers, consultants, and support professionals develops dashboards, forecasting tools, and customized analytics solutions for clients across multiple industries.

Northstar operates with a hybrid workforce and emphasizes responsible data use, collaboration, information security, and evidence-based decision-making. Every employee contributes to these commitments through accurate work, respectful communication, careful handling of information, and timely escalation of risks.

### How we work

- **Client focus.** Understand the business question, protect client trust, and deliver work that is accurate, useful, and appropriately documented.

- **Responsible data use.** Use data only for authorized purposes, apply the required safeguards, and raise concerns about quality, bias, privacy, or misuse.

- **Collaborative execution.** Share context, document decisions, invite informed challenge, and meet commitments across locations and time zones.

- **Evidence-based judgment.** Distinguish facts from assumptions, validate material conclusions, and communicate uncertainty rather than overstating confidence.

## 3 Employment Principles

### Equal employment opportunity

Northstar Analytics provides equal employment opportunity and makes employment decisions without unlawful discrimination. This commitment applies to recruiting, hiring, assignment, compensation, training, promotion, discipline, benefits, leave, and separation. Protected characteristics include race, color, religion, creed, sex, pregnancy, childbirth and related conditions, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, national origin, ancestry, age, disability, genetic information, military or veteran status, marital or family status, and any other status protected by applicable law.

### Reasonable accommodations

Northstar provides reasonable accommodations for qualified individuals with disabilities, pregnancy-related needs, sincerely held religious beliefs, and other circumstances protected by law, unless doing so would create an undue hardship. Employees may request an accommodation from People Operations or a manager without using special words. Northstar will engage in an interactive process, may request information permitted by law, and will keep medical information separate and confidential to the extent required.

### Employment relationship

Employment is at will where permitted by law. This means either the employee or Northstar may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without advance notice and with or without cause, subject to applicable law. No manager or representative may change the at-will relationship except through a written agreement signed by the employee and an authorized company officer.

### Employment classifications

| Classification | General meaning |

| --- | --- |

| Regular full time | An employee regularly scheduled for the full-time hours established for the role and location. |

| Regular part time | An employee regularly scheduled for fewer than the full-time hours established for the role and location. |

| Temporary | An employee hired for a defined period, project, season, or business need. Temporary status does not guarantee employment through the anticipated end date. |

| Exempt | An employee classified as exempt from overtime requirements under applicable law based on duties and pay requirements. |

| Nonexempt | An employee eligible for overtime and other wage-hour protections under applicable law. |

Classification affects timekeeping, overtime eligibility, and benefit eligibility. A manager may not change an employee's classification. Employees should contact People Operations if they believe their classification or pay practices are incorrect.

### Work authorization and background information

Employees must provide accurate information during the hiring process and complete work-authorization documentation within legally required timeframes. When permitted and appropriate for a role, Northstar may conduct background checks or verify credentials with required notice and authorization. Material falsification or omission may result in withdrawal of an offer or corrective action, up to and including separation.

### Personnel records

Employees must promptly update their address, telephone number, emergency contact, tax withholding, legal name, dependent information, and other employment records through the approved system or People Operations. Access to personnel records is limited based on business need and applicable law.

## 4 Work Arrangements and Timekeeping

### Work location and scheduling

A role may be designated onsite, remote, or hybrid based on client needs, team effectiveness, security, legal requirements, and the nature of the work. Work location is not guaranteed and may change with reasonable notice where practicable. Employees must follow the Remote and Hybrid Work Policy and obtain approval before changing their regular work location.

Employees may not work from another country, even temporarily, without advance written approval under the International Remote Work Policy. International work can create immigration, tax, employment, client-contract, export-control, insurance, and information-security obligations.

### Attendance and availability

- **Reliable attendance.** Report to work as scheduled and be available during required working and collaboration hours.

- **Prompt notice.** Notify the manager as soon as practical when an absence, delay, outage, or emergency affects work.

- **Calendar accuracy.** Maintain current availability and location information using approved tools, while avoiding sensitive personal details.

- **Client commitments.** Coordinate coverage for deadlines, meetings, and support responsibilities before planned time away.

### Timekeeping and overtime

Nonexempt employees must accurately record all time worked, meal periods where required, and leave using the approved system. Off-the-clock work is prohibited. Employees must not alter, omit, or ask another person to alter time records. Overtime should be approved in advance, but all time actually worked must still be reported and will be paid as required by law. Failure to obtain approval may lead to corrective action but will not result in withholding earned pay.

### Meal and rest periods

Northstar provides meal and rest periods in accordance with applicable law. Nonexempt employees must take, record, and remain relieved of work during unpaid meal periods when required. Employees should promptly report missed, interrupted, or late breaks to their manager or People Operations.

### Remote workspace expectations

- **Secure workspace.** Prevent unauthorized viewing, listening, access, or removal of company and client information.

- **Safe workspace.** Maintain a reasonably safe, functional work area and report work-related injuries immediately.

- **Reliable connectivity.** Maintain the connectivity reasonably required for the role and use approved secure access methods.

- **Professional environment.** Use appropriate backgrounds, audio practices, and meeting conduct when interacting with clients or colleagues.

## 5 Compensation and Business Expenses

### Pay practices

Employees are paid according to the payroll schedule communicated through their offer materials or the approved payroll system. Required taxes, authorized deductions, and benefit contributions will be withheld. Employees should review each pay statement and report suspected errors promptly. Northstar will investigate and correct confirmed errors in accordance with applicable law.

### Salary and wage information

Compensation decisions may consider role scope, skills, experience, performance, internal equity, market conditions, work location, and legal requirements. Pay changes must be approved through the designated process. Nothing in this handbook prohibits employees from discussing their own or coworkers' wages, hours, or working conditions or from choosing not to engage in those discussions.

### Business expenses

Northstar reimburses reasonable and necessary business expenses that meet the Business Expense Reimbursement Policy. Employees must obtain required approvals, use preferred suppliers or payment methods when directed, submit complete and accurate documentation, and meet stated deadlines. Employees should not approve their own expenses or divide transactions to avoid approval limits.

### Business travel

Travel must have a legitimate business purpose and be authorized, booked, conducted, and documented under the Business Travel and Booking Policy. Travelers must act professionally, protect company and client information, follow safety guidance, and promptly report disruptions or incidents that affect the trip or work.

## 6 Time Away From Work

### Paid time off

Eligible employees may take paid time off in accordance with the Paid Time Off Policy. That policy governs accrual or grants, requests, approvals, recording, carryover, permitted uses, and treatment at separation. Employees should request foreseeable time off as early as practical and coordinate coverage. Approval depends on staffing and business needs, except where leave is protected by law.

### Sick and protected time

Northstar provides sick time and other protected time off as required by applicable law. Employees should notify their manager or People Operations according to the applicable call-in procedure unless an emergency prevents notice. Northstar will not require employees to disclose a medical diagnosis beyond information permitted by law.

### Family medical and caregiver leave

Eligible employees may take job-protected or company-provided leave for qualifying medical, family, parental, military, caregiver, bereavement, civic, or other reasons. The Family and Medical Leave Policy and Parental and Caregiver Leave Policy explain eligibility, notice, documentation, benefits, job protection, and return-to-work requirements. State or local law may provide additional or more generous rights.

### Requesting leave

- **Foreseeable need.** For foreseeable FMLA leave, provide at least 30 days' notice when possible; otherwise provide notice as soon as practicable. For other foreseeable leave, notify the manager and People Operations as soon as the need and approximate timing are known.

- **Unexpected need.** Notify the manager or People Operations as soon as practical and follow normal absence reporting unless circumstances prevent it.

- **Documentation.** Provide timely, complete documentation that is permitted and reasonably required to evaluate the request.

- **Changes.** Report material changes to expected dates, work restrictions, or return-to-work status.

## 8 Workplace Conduct

Employees must act lawfully, ethically, respectfully, and professionally in every work setting. The Workplace Conduct and Professional Standards document provides additional requirements. Conduct may be reviewed even when it occurs off duty if it has a sufficient connection to work, company systems, client relationships, safety, or Northstar's legitimate business interests, subject to applicable law.

### Core standards

- **Integrity and accuracy.** Provide truthful information, preserve required records, correct material errors, and never fabricate analysis, credentials, time, expenses, or approvals.

- **Professional treatment.** Communicate respectfully, honor reasonable boundaries, and address disagreement without intimidation, ridicule, or personal attacks.

- **Conflicts of interest.** Disclose outside work, financial interests, personal relationships, gifts, or other circumstances that could impair or appear to impair objective judgment.

- **Legal and contractual compliance.** Follow laws, client commitments, licenses, confidentiality obligations, and company approval limits relevant to the work.

- **No improper payments.** Do not offer, request, authorize, or accept bribes, kickbacks, facilitation payments, or other improper benefits.

- **Substance and impairment.** Do not work while impaired in a manner that creates a safety, performance, or judgment risk; follow applicable drug, alcohol, and prescription-safety requirements.

### Confidentiality

Employees may learn confidential information about Northstar, clients, employees, applicants, vendors, or business partners. Access and use are limited to authorized business purposes. Confidentiality obligations continue after employment ends. These obligations do not prohibit legally protected reporting, cooperation with government agencies, discussion of working conditions, or other protected activity.

### Corrective action

Policy or performance concerns may result in coaching, training, warnings, reassignment, suspension, access restrictions, or separation. Northstar may choose the response it considers appropriate based on the circumstances and is not required to follow a fixed sequence, subject to applicable law and the at-will relationship.

## 9 Respectful Workplace

### Prohibited discrimination and harassment

Northstar prohibits discrimination, harassment, and retaliation based on a protected characteristic. Prohibited conduct may be verbal, visual, physical, written, or digital and may involve employees, managers, clients, contractors, vendors, or visitors. Conduct can violate policy even when it does not violate law, occur repeatedly, or involve an intent to offend.

- Examples include slurs, stereotypes, insults, threats, exclusion, unwanted sexual attention, repeated unwelcome comments, offensive images or messages, and employment decisions based on protected status.

- Sexual harassment includes unwelcome sexual advances, requests for sexual favors, sexual or gender-based conduct, and conditioning work opportunities on submission to or rejection of such conduct.

- Retaliation includes adverse treatment because a person raised a concern, supported another person, requested an accommodation, or participated in an inquiry or proceeding.

### Reporting

Employees should report concerns promptly through any channel listed in Section 13. Employees are not required to report to a person involved in the concern or to confront that person. Managers who receive or observe possible discrimination, harassment, or retaliation must promptly notify People Operations, even if the employee asks the manager to keep the matter informal or confidential.

### Response

Northstar will assess reports promptly, take interim protective measures when appropriate, conduct a fair review, reach findings based on available information, and take responsive action. Information will be shared only with those who need it for a lawful or business purpose, but complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. See the Anti-Harassment and Discrimination Policy and Workplace Concern and HR Case Procedure for details.

## 10 Information Security and Responsible Data Use

Protecting company, client, employee, and partner information is a condition of access to Northstar systems and data. Employees must follow the Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy, Data Classification and Handling Standard, and Security Incident Reporting Procedure.

### Access and authentication

- **Use individual accounts.** Do not share passwords, multifactor authentication prompts, tokens, keys, or access badges.

- **Use least privilege.** Access only the information and systems needed for authorized work and request removal of access that is no longer needed.

- **Protect credentials.** Use approved password and authentication tools, and report unexpected prompts or suspected compromise immediately.

- **Lock and secure.** Lock screens when unattended and secure devices and papers during storage, travel, and disposal.

### Data handling

Employees must identify the applicable data classification and follow its controls for collection, access, storage, transmission, retention, sharing, and disposal. Company or client data may be stored only in approved locations. Employees must not send restricted information to personal accounts, place it in unapproved collaboration tools, or copy it to unauthorized devices or media.

### Responsible analytics and artificial intelligence

- **Approved tools and purposes.** Use analytics and AI systems only when approved for the relevant data, client, and business purpose.

- **Data minimization.** Use the minimum data reasonably necessary and remove or mask sensitive fields when required.

- **Human accountability.** Review outputs for accuracy, bias, privacy, security, explainability, and fitness for the intended decision.

- **No unsupported claims.** Do not present generated or modeled output as verified fact without appropriate validation and documentation.

- **Escalate high impact uses.** Obtain required review before using automated outputs in employment, eligibility, pricing, safety, legal, health, or similarly consequential decisions.

### Security incident reporting

Immediately report suspected phishing, malware, lost or stolen equipment, accidental disclosure, unauthorized access, misdirected messages, unusual authentication activity, or other possible security incidents. Do not delay reporting while trying to determine severity or fix the issue alone. Preserve relevant information and follow instructions from Information Security.

## 11 Company Resources and Communications

### Company equipment

Company equipment is assigned for authorized business use and must be protected, maintained, and returned under the Company Equipment Lifecycle Policy and Procedures. Employees may not disable security controls, install unapproved software, allow unauthorized users to use equipment, or repair or dispose of equipment outside approved processes.

### Acceptable use and privacy

Limited personal use of company systems may be permitted when it is lawful, reasonable, does not interfere with work, creates no material cost or risk, and complies with policy. Employees should not expect personal privacy when using company systems, accounts, networks, or devices. Northstar may access, preserve, review, or monitor company resources for security, support, legal, compliance, and business-continuity purposes, subject to applicable law.

### Electronic communications

- **Use approved channels.** Conduct company business through approved email, messaging, file-sharing, project, and meeting systems.

- **Protect recipients and content.** Verify addresses, permissions, links, attachments, and classification before sending or sharing.

- **Maintain professionalism.** Write with the same care expected in an in-person workplace and preserve required business records.

- **Avoid unauthorized recording.** Do not record meetings, calls, screens, or workspaces without required consent and approval.

### Public statements and social media

Only authorized representatives may speak for Northstar or disclose nonpublic company or client information. Employees speaking personally should not imply company endorsement and must follow confidentiality, intellectual-property, respectful-workplace, and client obligations. This section does not restrict protected communications about wages, hours, working conditions, or other legally protected matters.

## 12 Performance and Development

Northstar expects ongoing alignment between role responsibilities, measurable goals, client commitments, quality standards, collaboration, and professional growth. New employees must complete the activities in the Employee Onboarding Guide, including account setup, security and compliance training, policy acknowledgments, introductions, and role-specific learning. All employees must complete recurring or assigned training by the stated deadline.

## 13 Reporting Concerns and Non Retaliation

Northstar relies on prompt reporting to address concerns before harm increases. Employees may raise a good-faith concern even when they do not have complete evidence or are unsure whether a policy was violated. Knowingly making a false report is prohibited, but a report that is not substantiated is not false merely because evidence is inconclusive.

### Reporting channels

| Channel | Appropriate use |

| --- | --- |

| Manager or another manager | Routine workplace concerns, conduct, workload, policy questions, or when the direct manager is unavailable or involved. |

| People Operations | Employment, leave, accommodation, discrimination, harassment, retaliation, pay, performance, or sensitive workplace concerns. |

| Information Security | Phishing, lost devices, unauthorized access, data exposure, malware, suspicious activity, or security-control failures. |

| Legal or executive leadership | Serious legal, ethical, financial, client, or leadership concerns when another channel is unsuitable. |

| Emergency services | Immediate threats to life, safety, or property; contact local emergency services first, then notify Northstar when safe. |

Employees may use any available channel and are not required to follow the reporting line in sequence. The Workplace Concern and HR Case Procedure explains intake, urgency assessment, documentation, escalation, confidentiality, and case handling.

### Cooperation and confidentiality

Employees must cooperate honestly in authorized reviews and preserve relevant information. Participants should protect the integrity and privacy of the process, but Northstar does not prohibit lawful discussion of workplace conditions or communication with government agencies. Employees must not interfere with an inquiry, coach witnesses, destroy records, or retaliate.

### Non retaliation

Retaliation is prohibited against anyone who in good faith reports a concern, seeks guidance, requests an accommodation or leave, refuses suspected unlawful conduct, participates in an inquiry, or exercises a protected right. Employees should report suspected retaliation immediately so protective action can be considered.

## 14 Safety and Emergency Response

Northstar is committed to a safe and secure work environment. Employees must follow site and client safety rules, use equipment as instructed, maintain safe remote workspaces, and promptly report work-related injuries, illnesses, hazards, threats, and near misses. In an immediate emergency, move to safety and contact local emergency services before notifying Northstar.

### Workplace violence

Threats, intimidation, stalking, fighting, deliberate property damage, and acts of violence are prohibited. Weapons are prohibited on company premises, at company events, in company vehicles, and while conducting company business except as required by applicable law or expressly authorized for a legitimate business reason. Employees should not place themselves in danger to investigate or intervene.

### Injuries and workers compensation

Employees must report work-related injuries or illnesses as soon as practical, even if they appear minor or occur while working remotely or at a client location. People Operations will provide information about medical care, documentation, workers' compensation, leave, and return-to-work steps as applicable.

### Business continuity

- **Follow alerts.** Monitor approved company communication channels during severe weather, outages, civil emergencies, or site closures.

- **Protect people first.** Do not travel or remain in an unsafe setting merely to meet a work commitment.

- **Protect information.** Maintain security controls during alternate work arrangements and report any loss of control over devices or data.

- **Coordinate priorities.** Confirm availability, client impacts, recovery tasks, and decision authority with the manager.

## 15 Separation From Employment

### Voluntary separation

Employees who choose to resign are requested to provide written notice to their manager and People Operations. Advance notice supports transition planning but does not change the at-will relationship. Northstar may accept a resignation immediately or set an earlier last day, subject to applicable law.

### Company initiated separation

Northstar may end employment because of performance, conduct, attendance, business conditions, role changes, loss of work authorization, or other lawful reasons. The company will provide notices, final pay, and benefit information as required by law and applicable plans.

### Transition obligations

- **Return property.** Return company and client equipment, records, badges, keys, payment cards, storage media, and other assets by the deadline provided.

- **Preserve information.** Do not delete, copy, transfer, or retain company or client information except as expressly authorized.

- **Complete handoff.** Provide current work status, locations of approved records, known risks, and reasonable transition information.

- **Protect access.** Stop using company accounts and credentials when access is removed; do not attempt to bypass deactivation.

- **Continue obligations.** Honor surviving confidentiality, intellectual-property, cooperation, and other lawful post-employment obligations.

### Company equipment and expenses

Equipment return is governed by the Company Equipment Lifecycle Policy and Procedures. Outstanding business expenses should be submitted promptly under the Business Expense Reimbursement Policy. Northstar will not make deductions from final pay unless authorized and permitted by law.
